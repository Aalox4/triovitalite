❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

Yes! We accept reservations for parties of 6 or more. For smaller groups, walk-ins are always welcome. You can book through our website or give us a call.

Do you offer gluten-free options?

Absolutely! We have gluten-free buns and several gluten-free menu items. Just let your server know about any dietary restrictions.

Do you cater for events?

Yes! We offer catering for parties, corporate events, and special occasions. Contact us for a custom quote.

Is there parking available?

We have a free parking lot behind the restaurant with 20 spaces. Street parking is also available nearby.

Do you deliver?

We deliver through our own drivers within a 3-mile radius. You can also find us on UberEats, DoorDash, and Grubhub.
